Our Mission

Empowering marginalized and excluded groups through customized social and technical solutions.

Access Edutech Private Limited is a social development venture dedicated to designing customized consulting and technical solutions. With a pool of experienced professionals who have worked alongside national, international, and bilateral organizations, we strengthen development initiatives and create pathways for inclusive growth.

Our Key Verticals

Comprehensive consulting, training, and technology-driven solutions across development sectors.

Development Solutions

Strategic consulting, project management, and institutional strengthening for non-profits and government bodies.

ICT4 Development

Deploying smart classrooms, digital innovation labs, and open-source enterprise tools to bridge the digital divide.

Employable Skills

Industry-aligned vocational training, IT literacy, and placement support to boost youth employability.

Health & Education

Capacity building for educators and community health initiatives aimed at improving grassroots outcomes.

Productive Livelihood

Supporting rural entrepreneurship, market linkages, and sustainable income generation models.
